# Authorization Corpus v4

ERSEC 29.1.1 treats the authorization corpus as a canonical research artifact. Case declarations are hashed after deterministic normalization. The manifest records case families, truth classes, and relationship dimensions.

Relationship graph metadata is descriptive: an identity/tenant/resource relationship does not itself establish that the application enforced the relationship.

Ambiguous and observation-unavailable cases remain visible and are excluded from precision/recall. Benchmark results from this corpus are controlled measurements and must not be presented as general-world accuracy claims without external validation.
